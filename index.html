This is an educational estimate, not a diagnosis. A1C and average glucose can be affected by conditions like anemia. Always interpret your results with a doctor.

How it works

  1. Enter either number — your A1C percentage, or your average glucose meter reading.
  2. We apply the standard formula (eAG = 28.7 × A1C − 46.7), adopted by the ADA to help translate lab A1C results into the same units as a home glucose meter.
  3. We show where it falls against normal, prediabetes, and diabetes reference ranges.

A1C reflects a 2-3 month average, while a glucose meter shows a single moment in time — they answer different questions and both matter.
